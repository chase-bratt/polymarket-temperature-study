## Headline

1. **follow's record is clean (30/30 since the bid filter, +$8.54) but its wins rest on the forecast, not the
   thermometer.** Of the 30 settled post-filter trades, only 1 was settled by observation alone; the rest depended on
   max(4 models, remaining hours) + 1.5 °C holding. Open positions are not scored here (they are not data until they
   settle), but Oct 25 shows the cap can fail: in Chengdu the bot's own feasible range moved after entry, leaving it
   holding NO 25°C and YES 25°C on the same bucket (one side must lose), and in Kuala Lumpur and Wuhan the observed
   max passed the YES bucket (a daily max cannot come back down). All passed the bid ≥ 0.50 filter. follow as built is
   mostly a same-day forecast bet with a 19:1 payoff, which is the bet intraday was retired for.
2. **forecast's +24 % ± 32 % overstates the evidence.** 30 trades, but 24 of them on one event date and all on two
   dates; the band assumes independence, and same-date trades share the weather regime (Seoul and Busan both lost
   with z ≈ −1.5; Beijing and Wuhan both won with z ≈ −2.3). Effective sample is a handful of dates.
3. **The thesis behind forecast is nevertheless the most promising thing in the bot.** Since the log-pool went live
   (two epochs, 60 NO-on-favourite trades, 5 dates) the market's favourite bucket hit 15 times vs 24.4 implied by the
   NO price and 15.3 implied by the pooled model. The model's number matched reality in both epochs; the market's
   did not. Unproven, but consistent and measured against executable prices.

## Changes I am confident in (none raise risk)

1. **follow: trade only what the thermometer has already settled.** Drop F from `feasible_range` during the day:
   highs R_hi = +∞, lows R_lo = −∞ (keep F for nothing). Effect: same-day highs buy NO only on buckets below the
   observed max (hi_j < R_lo); lows the mirror; YES only when the day is over with full coverage, or on the open-ended
   end bucket once the observation is inside it. Fewer trades, but every one rests on the strategy's actual thesis.
   This removes the dead→locked flips that produced the Chengdu both-sides position. Code change → bump
   `follow_version`. Governance: follow is frozen until 2026-10-30; this is a structural fix, not a tune, so it is
   your call whether it waits for the weekly review.
2. **Governance: cluster the error band by event date** (SE from per-date mean returns, or date × region), show
   distinct-date count next to n, and require ≥ 15 distinct dates before any verdict. Measurement only, no trading
   effect. Today forecast would read "2 dates, too few".
3. **Scorecard: label forecast trades by type** (NO-favourite / YES / other) and report hit rate of the market's
   favourite vs its implied probability. That is the single number the forecast strategy lives or dies on; today it
   has to be reconstructed from the CSV.
4. **Never hold both sides of one bucket in a strategy.** `db.position` only blocks the same side; blocking the
   opposite side is a one-line guard. (Becomes unreachable for follow after change 1, but it is cheap insurance.)

## Research, not changes (do before any parameter move)

- **Market-distribution construction.** `p_mkt` uses the ask alone where a bucket has no bid; with ~73 % overround
  on asks, normalising inflates the tails and deflates the favourite, which pushes the pool toward "favourite
  overpriced". Re-score the pool OOS with no-bid buckets set to ask/2 or to the floor. If the pool's OOS gain and
  the NO-favourite hit-rate gap survive, the thesis is real rather than an artefact of the de-vig.
- **Market calibration by market-prob bin** on liquid favourites from the existing snapshots: the direct, trade-free
  test of "modal bucket overpriced". If favourites at 0.45 win 45 %, forecast has no edge and the 60-trade result is noise.
- Keep the +0.7 °C bias and 0.8 inflation until the 50-station backfill test; two cold dates are not evidence.

## What is working and should not be touched

Paper-only, tiny caps, epoch scoring, weekly-only parameter changes, retirement being permanent, keeping the old
losing data as the baseline. The bot's main asset is the data capture and the discipline, not the current P/L.
