## Blocking findings

### A1: CO-006 deletes the standing preregistration

**P0; CONFIRMED DEFECT; high confidence; introduced by this diff.** The patch replaces all 96 existing lines of `notes/PREREGISTRATION.md` with a 19-line section 7, removing the earlier rules while continuing to reference section 2 and supersede section 6. The test confirms sections 1–5 are absent. Evidence: Carbon `CO-006.diff`, hunk `@@ -1,96 +1,19 @@`; post-patch `notes/PREREGISTRATION.md:1–19`; `test_safety_preregistration_preserves_sections_1_to_5`, 2026-11-16.

Inference: registration can faithfully hash an incomplete rulebook, so a clean manifest does not prove governance completeness. The strongest opposing explanation is an accidental diff-generation error rather than an intended deletion; either way, this exact artifact must not be applied unchanged. Requested amendment: preserve the existing text and append an explicit, approved amendment; remove the premature “approved by Chase” assertion until approval is actually recorded.

### A2: “READY” does not require the new blend gate

**P0; CONFIRMED DEFECT; high confidence; CO-006 integration omission.** `scoring.go_live()` still evaluates the old checklist and does not incorporate `blend_evaluation()` or manifest-integrity flags. A synthetic fixture of 100 profitable settled trades across 20 dates returns `ready=True`, while the new blend evaluator returns `PENDING (0.0/30.0 effective dates)`. Evidence: post-patch `polybot/scoring.py:450–473`; `test_safety_ready_requires_new_blend_gate` and its recorded observations, 2026-11-16.

Inference: the deterministic report can offer contradictory promotion guidance. This is not proof of automatic real-money activation: the preregistration expressly retains human approval, and the fixture supplies a qualifying baseline rather than recreating a complete production history. Requested amendment: make blend readiness conjunctive with the new final evaluation, valid integrity state, and existing operational checks; missing inputs must fail closed.

### A3: Event/open/cash checks exclude fees

**P0; CONFIRMED DEFECT; high confidence; inherited execution issue retained by CO-006.** At ask 0.78, five-share minimum, and the 1.02 sizing buffer, the evaluator requests 3.978 USDC before fees. The real `PaperBroker` fixture fills 5.1 shares, charges 0.04376 USDC, records all-in exposure of 4.02176 against a 4.00 event cap, and leaves cash at −0.04376 when starting cash is 3.978. Evidence: post-patch `polybot/blend.py:197–214`, `polybot/broker.py:22–72`; `test_safety_fee_inclusive_event_cap_and_cash`, 2026-11-16.

The fee calculation is not a flat 5% of stake: the venue documents `shares × feeRate × price × (1 − price)` and five-decimal rounding; weather is listed at feeRate 0.05, subject to market parameters ([Polymarket official fee documentation, published 2026-11-12, retrieved 2026-11-16](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees)).

Inference: lowering the quote ceiling to 0.78 does not make the 4.00 cap fee-safe. The opposing explanation that the cap means pre-fee principal is inconsistent with `db.event_exposure()` accumulating cost plus fee, and does not solve negative cash. Requested amendment: calculate or reserve all-in spend from executable depth, resize or reject before writing, and enforce the invariant at the broker boundary as well as in the strategy.

### A4: Closure can trigger payout without final resolution

**P0; CONFIRMED DEFECT; high confidence for code behavior, medium for production reachability; inherited settlement issue.** A mocked market with `closed=true`, absent UMA status, and prices `[0.6,0.4]` settles a held YES position and pays 10 USDC for ten shares. The code treats absent resolution status as acceptable and selects a winner using `price > 0.5`. Evidence: post-patch `polybot/settle.py:24–35`; `test_safety_settlement_requires_final_resolution`, 2026-11-16.

The venue defines `closed` as “resolved or been closed,” not as affirmative payout finality ([Polymarket official market details, published 2026-11-12, retrieved 2026-11-16](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/market-details)). The strongest opposing explanation is that the weather-market API normally supplies only final binary prices for closed markets; no captured current response set established that guarantee here.

Requested amendment: require explicit authoritative finality and validated payout semantics; quarantine unknown, disputed, malformed, or unsupported outcomes rather than guessing from market price. Do not assume a generic split-payout rule applies to every negative-risk weather contract.

## Conditional and lower-priority findings

- **A5, blend-only outcome collection:** P1, CONFIRMED conditional defect, high confidence. Full settlement polls held markets or markets present in legacy `snapshots`, but not unheld markets present only in `blend_snaps`; the two-bucket fixture makes zero fetches. This can omit evaluation outcomes if legacy snapshot coverage is absent. It does not prove the current scorer always stalls: a passing control confirms retirement alone does not stop legacy forecast snapshotting. Evidence: `polybot/settle.py:12–16`, `test_safety_blend_only_unheld_buckets_receive_outcomes`, and `test_control_retired_forecast_still_snapshots`, 2026-11-16.
- **A6, maker trade direction:** P1 before maker reuse or reliance on historical maker realism; CONFIRMED filter defect, high confidence. `_qualifying()` counts ten shares of an own-outcome BUY print at a resting bid as qualifying fill volume because it does not filter trade direction. This is a retired maker path, not the active blend taker path, and counting volume is not itself proof that a position filled after queue deductions. Evidence: `polybot/execution.py:99–108`; `test_safety_maker_buy_print_not_sell_liquidity`, 2026-11-16.
- **A7, future cache timestamps:** P1 for replay validity, bounded live risk; CONFIRMED missing guard, high confidence. `blend.inputs()` accepts a cache row fetched one day after the supplied decision timestamp because negative age satisfies `age < blend_refresh_min`; the fixture returns age −1440 minutes. This establishes a leakage-enabling replay path, not observed live leakage. Evidence: `polybot/blend.py:68–86`; `test_safety_future_input_cache_is_rejected`, 2026-11-16.
- **A8, quota runbook versus epoch:** P1 documentation/governance amendment, high confidence. Changing `blend_refresh_min` from 55 to 110 changes the computed epoch hash even with an unchanged version string, from `08a090ebde` to `be7b676b7c` in the fixture. Carbon's quota-only runbook should not imply epoch continuity; preserve this separation rather than bypassing it to retain sample size. Evidence: Carbon specification's “24-Hour Quota Check”; `test_control_refresh_setting_changes_epoch`, 2026-11-16.
- **A9, partial-fill metadata:** P2 clarification, high confidence. A request for 10 USDC against six shares at 0.50 records actual cost 3.00 and the correct 0.075 fee, but order status is `filled`. Quantities and cash reconcile; if `filled` means fully satisfied rather than terminal, that metadata is misleading. Evidence: `polybot/broker.py:47–55`; `test_control_partial_fill_accounts_actual_quantity`, 2026-11-16. Define the intended status contract before changing downstream logic.
